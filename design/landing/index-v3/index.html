Why it matters

Safety shouldn't end when the shift does

Night shifts, long commutes and unreported incidents leave too many women navigating risk alone. SHIFTSAFE closes the gaps between workplace, road and home.

Every ride

Silence

Fear of exposure keeps harassment hidden. Women need safe, confidential channels that organisations can act on.

Privacy & consent

You decide who sees what

SHIFTSAFE is built on consent. Nothing is shared with your organisation or your trusted contacts unless you allow it — and you can change your mind anytime.

Granular controls

Set permissions per contact and per feature — journeys, location, shift times.

Anonymous by design

Anonymous complaints are built not to store or expose your identity.

Access you can audit

Role-based access controls and logs of who viewed sensitive information.

Revoke anytime

Withdraw any permission you've given, whenever you choose.

Location only when authorised

Collected only during a journey you share or an SOS.
